# Risk and analytics

Pre-trade risk remains internal and evaluates eligibility, instrument/market state, freshness, quantity/notional, funds/position limits, loss limits, and trading controls. Provider risk signals may supplement but not override policy.

Portfolio exposure, allocation, volatility, beta, drawdown, and VaR require authoritative holdings and history. VaR must state model, confidence, horizon, inputs, and limitations. Until those inputs are certified these outputs are documented gaps, not estimates. Margin and liquidation remain disabled pending authoritative collateral contracts.

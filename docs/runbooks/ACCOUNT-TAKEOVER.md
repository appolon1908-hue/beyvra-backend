# Account takeover

1. Confirm alert scope without copying PII into chat or tickets.
2. Freeze the account, revoke sessions, and preserve security/audit events.
3. Route the fraud case to Security; do not assert IP geolocation as fact.
4. Require authenticated recovery and MFA reset policy. Unfreeze through maker/checker.
5. Review notifications, downstream attempts, and audit integrity; record safe closure evidence.

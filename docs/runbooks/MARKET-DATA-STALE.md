# Market Data Stale

Symptoms: age exceeds policy or stale counter rises. Dashboard: Platform/Trading. Alert: market freshness alert. Verify approved provider status, governance decision, last accepted update, and request latency. Keep order risk denial active; do not bypass governance or activate disabled providers. Roll back feed changes. Escalate market-data/platform. Confirm freshness within policy before simulated acceptance resumes.

Do not infer or fabricate prices. Restore the authority snapshot and resume only after freshness policy passes.

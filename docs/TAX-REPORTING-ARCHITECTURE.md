# Tax and reporting architecture

`TaxReportingProvider` supports cost-basis calculation and versioned tax-lot/document generation. Internal reports derive from immutable authoritative trades, transfers, and Financial Service records. Provider artifacts retain version, inputs, provider reference, generation time, and audit provenance.

TaxBit and Ledgible are unapproved candidates. No live filing, tax advice, or legal/tax correctness claim is made.

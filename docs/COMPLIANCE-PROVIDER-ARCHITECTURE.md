# Compliance provider architecture

`ComplianceProvider` covers verification sessions, identity screening, status, and health. Persona and Alloy are candidate adapters only. Vendor results are evidence inputs; Beyvra policy is final authority for KYC, AML, sanctions, jurisdiction, eligibility, overrides, and manual review.

Fixtures must cover approved, pending, rejected, manual review, outage, and replay. No vendor approval or legal conclusion is asserted.
